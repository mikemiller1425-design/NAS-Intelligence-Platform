## The decision being requested

What exact wording and severity do Sentinel alerts use?

## Why it matters

Alert phrasing is a Sentinel deliverable. The Sentinel is deliberately late in the ladder, so this costs almost nothing on the critical path.

## Safe options

**1. Terse structured alerts**

Machine-parseable prefix plus a short human phrase.

*Tradeoff:* Least ambiguous; easiest to route.

**2. Prose alerts**

Full sentences.

*Tradeoff:* Friendlier, but harder to filter and more likely to leak private filenames.

## Recommendation

**Option 1**, with an explicit rule that alerts never contain private filenames — the security specification already requires payloads to avoid unnecessary private filenames in alerts.

## What the operator must supply

Preferred phrasing and severity vocabulary.

## Default behaviour if unresolved

The Sentinel rung stops. Nothing else is affected.

## Evidence required to close

An agreed alert catalogue.

## Cost of changing the decision later

Very low.
