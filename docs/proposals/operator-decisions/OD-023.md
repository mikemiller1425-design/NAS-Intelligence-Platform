## The decision being requested

May a dry-run plan generated without a measured destination descriptor ever be promoted to execution?

## Why it matters

A capability descriptor is valid only if measured. Measuring a **destination** descriptor requires a write to that destination. G4 authorizes no write to any NAS path. So a G4 plan is structurally incapable of carrying a valid destination descriptor — yet a plan must carry one at approval.

## Safe options

**1. G4 plans are descriptor-incomplete and non-promotable; destination characterization opens G5**

The dry run produces a plan for review, not for execution. The first G5 activity measures the destination, after which a promotable plan is produced.

*Tradeoff:* Clean and safe. Costs one extra planning cycle. **This is what the Build Ladder currently assumes.**

**2. Allow a narrowly scoped destination probe at G4**

Permit one write into a designated scratch area inside the destination tree, solely to characterize.

*Tradeoff:* Breaks the read-only guarantee that makes G4 safe to authorize. A scratch write is still a write to a NAS destination.

**3. Characterize the destination from the source descriptor by inference**

Assume destination capabilities match the source, or a documented profile.

*Tradeoff:* **Not viable.** Assumed descriptors are explicitly invalid; the whole point of measurement is that assumption is what fails.

## Recommendation

**Option 1.** It is the only option that preserves G4's read-only guarantee. The Build Ladder already assumes it and states the assumption rather than deciding it — this brief asks you to confirm or replace that assumption.

## What the operator must supply

Confirmation that G4 plans are non-promotable, or a different resolution.

## Default behaviour if unresolved

The dry-run execution rung stops, because it cannot state whether its output is promotable.

## Evidence required to close

A recorded decision on plan promotability at G4.

## Cost of changing the decision later

Moderate. Changing it later would change what a dry run is for.
