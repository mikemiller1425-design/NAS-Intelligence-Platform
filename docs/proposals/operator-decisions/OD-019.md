## The decision being requested

Which stack does the review console use?

## Why it matters

The console is a non-executing decision surface and is deliberately the second-to-last capability rung. The decision costs nothing on the critical path.

## Safe options

**1. Local HTML plus a small local API**

Smallest surface; no build toolchain.

*Tradeoff:* Least capable UI, but the console is intentionally limited.

**2. React/Next plus FastAPI**

The direction already sketched in the technology decision.

*Tradeoff:* Larger dependency surface for a component that must remain minimal.

## Recommendation

**Option 1** unless the operator specifically wants a richer UI. The console must remain provably redundant to the CLI, and a smaller stack makes that easier to demonstrate.

## What the operator must supply

The chosen stack.

## Default behaviour if unresolved

The console rung stops. Everything else proceeds; the CLI remains the primary surface.

## Evidence required to close

A recorded stack choice.

## Cost of changing the decision later

Low.
