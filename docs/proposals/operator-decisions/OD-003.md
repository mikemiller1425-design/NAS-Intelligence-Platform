## The decision being requested

Is any person-identity assistance permitted, and under what confirmation requirements?

## Why it matters

Identity signals would be extracted from real media at the first live read. The identity rule stays disabled until this resolves. This is a privacy decision, not a technical one.

## What the operator must supply

A written privacy policy stating whether identity inference is permitted at all, and if so what confirmation is mandatory.

## Default behaviour if unresolved

The identity rule remains disabled. Everything else proceeds.

## Evidence required to close

A written answer recorded against OD-003 in `docs/05-governance/open-decisions.md`, with the
supplying evidence attached.

## Register note (verbatim, for context)

> Whether any person-identity assistance is allowed; confirmation requirements. Identity signals would be extracted from real media at first live read. The identity rule stays disabled until this resolves.
