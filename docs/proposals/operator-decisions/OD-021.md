## The decision being requested

What counts as migration completeness, and what residual-exception tolerance is acceptable?

## Why it matters

Completion requires every in-scope item to reach a terminal disposition and residual exceptions to be owned — but nothing defines how many unresolved or failed items are tolerable, or who owns them.

## What the operator must supply

A tolerance threshold and an ownership rule.

## Default behaviour if unresolved

The reconciliation rung is implementable; its completion package is not.

## Evidence required to close

A written answer recorded against OD-021 in `docs/05-governance/open-decisions.md`, with the
supplying evidence attached.
