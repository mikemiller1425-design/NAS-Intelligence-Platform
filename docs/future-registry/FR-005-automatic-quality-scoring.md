# FR-005 — Automatic quality scoring

## Purpose
Score blur, exposure, or other quality metrics to help curation.

## Risks
Automatic deletion of “low quality” irreplaceable personal media; biased scoring.

## Dependencies
Non-destructive scoring pipeline; explicit human review before any discard proposal.

## Reason excluded from V1
Assignment excludes automatic deletion of low-quality media. Scoring that leads to deletion is unsafe for V1.

## Promotion condition
Scores are advisory only; discard proposals require separate approval; never default-enabled for private vaults.
