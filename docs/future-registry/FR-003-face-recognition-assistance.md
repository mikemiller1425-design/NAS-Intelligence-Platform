# FR-003 — Face-recognition assistance

## Purpose
Assist grouping of known people in media for portfolio organization.

## Dependencies
Explicit identity-recognition privacy policy (open decision OD-003); human confirmation workflow; disabled-by-default rules.

## Risks
Misidentification; sensitive private media exposure; treating a single detected person as certain identity.

## Reason excluded from V1
Assignment and operations manual require explicit policy before identity automation. V1 may record provisional candidate signals only under review.

## Promotion condition
Operator-approved privacy policy; confirmation-required rules; no auto-execution of identity destinations.
