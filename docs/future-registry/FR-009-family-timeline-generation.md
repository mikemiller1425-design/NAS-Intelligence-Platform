# FR-009 — Family timeline generation

## Purpose
Build chronological family timelines from dated media and events.

## Dependencies
Reliable capture-time metadata; optional face/person policy; organized family vault.

## Risks
Incorrect chronology; privacy of family members; identity assumptions.

## Reason excluded from V1
Post-organization storytelling feature; not needed for migration safety.

## Promotion condition
Family vault organized; date integrity validated; identity features separately approved.
