# Authority Order

Authority in the NAS Intelligence Platform is hierarchical and conservative.

1. Source manual and operator intent, when consistent with the active blueprint.
2. Active governance documents in this repository.
3. Approved immutable plans and their evidence bundles.
4. Verified operational output and reconciliation records.
5. Read-only sentinel observations.

## Authority rules
- The human operator is final authority for taxonomy, risky classifications, destructive actions, rollout gates, and policy changes.
- The Mac mini engine may propose and execute only within an approved plan.
- The Raspberry Pi sentinel may observe and alert, but it cannot classify, approve, mutate, or delete.
- Dashboards and reports are informational, not authoritative.
- If documents conflict, the stricter safety rule wins until the conflict is resolved.
